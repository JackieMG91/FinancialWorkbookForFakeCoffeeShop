# Coffee Shop Accounting & Financial Statements Workbook

An end-to-end Excel accounting workbook for a hypothetical coffee shop, demonstrating how raw journal entries flow from a General Ledger into a complete three-statement financial model.

## 📊 Workbook Structure
* **General Ledger:** Detailed log of business transactions, debits, credits, and account classifications.
* **Income Statement:** Tracks revenue, Cost of Goods Sold (COGS), operating expenses, and net income.
* **Balance Sheet:** Summarizes assets, liabilities, and owner's equity at a specific point in time.
* **Statement of Cash Flows:** Reconciles net income to cash flows from operating, investing, and financing activities.

## ✨ Highlights
* **Full Accounting Cycle:** Shows the direct link between daily bookkeeping entries and high-level financial reporting.
* **Dynamic Formulas:** Built with standard Excel lookup and aggregation formulas (`SUMIFS`, `INDEX/MATCH`, etc.) to ensure financial statements automatically update as ledger entries change.
* **Balanced Financials:** Structured to maintain the fundamental accounting equation ($Assets = Liabilities + Equity$) and cross-statement consistency.
* * **VBA Macros:** The general ledger has VBA macros for safe double-entry accounting and to show/hide the three 3 statesments for easy viewing.

## 🚀 How to Use
1. Clone the repository or download the `.xlsx` file directly.
2. Open the workbook in Microsoft Excel or Google Sheets.
3. Review the `General Ledger` tab to inspect the underlying transactions and see how they dynamically populate the three financial statements.
